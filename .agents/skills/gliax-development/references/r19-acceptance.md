# GLIAX R19 acceptance matrix — approved design goals, not yet implemented

1. Digital simulations: keep the "Simulaciones digitales" category in appropriate areas; clear selected/unselected control contrast; seven visually distinct realistic interface families rather than generic repeated tables; remove visible redundant "simulación" labels; preserve pedagogical meaning and accessibility.
2. Reading: for embedded HTML passage text, optional word/syllable color highlighting and optional visual reading guide, both reversible; do NOT overlay immersive reader on image-like tickets, posters, receipts or other visual documents. Avoid transforming document semantics or changing scoring.
3. "La palabra que encaja": explain whether a response meets stated semantic constraints. Accept reviewed valid synonyms/alternatives (e.g. "gorrión" for flying, tree-nesting animal). Clearly mark unverifiable answers as unverified, not incorrect; add tests for accepted/rejected/unverifiable.
4. "Palabras en caída": correct the wording so complete accurate words advance automatically; preserve accessible fallback and disable/clear inert controls after round completion.
5. From age 8, add a separate Singapur-style two-step word-problem family with concrete-pictorial-abstract support and bar models where appropriate. Record identifying intermediate datum, operation choice and final answer separately; progressively fade assistance; do not label generic arithmetic "Singapore method".
6. Remove "Geometría" as separate area selector. Audit tasks misclassified as geometry; place actual geometry under Mathematics and non-geometry according to trained construct. Keep historical IDs, progress and favorites accessible.
7. Remove the Digital Simulations selector and generator delivery from Lenguaje. Preserve appropriate language families and avoid stale mixed selectors.
8. Remove redundant "Volumen de práctica por sesión" line plot; retain exact session data and the time-activity plot. Compare performance by family only when same construct, level and metric version; never imply deterioration from fewer exercises.

Validation: targeted Node and browser tests, responsive and keyboard review, synthetic-account Edge test, historical-data compatibility and editorial review of regional Spanish. Do not publish until the coordinated staging release is explicitly authorized.
